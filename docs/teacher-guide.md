# Teacher Guide

## Choose a creation mode

### Quick Mode

Use this when the teacher already has the topic, names, facts, and images. Attach or edit the content pack and ask the AI to change content only. This is the fastest route and works well for a classroom draft.

### Deep Mode

Use this when accuracy and source review matter. The AI should ask about the learners, topic, language, number of items, sources, image rights, and desired difficulty before generating the pack. The teacher approves the candidate list and the final preview.

## Prepare a content pack

Each item needs:

- A unique `id`
- A Thai and/or English title
- A Thai and/or English field or category
- A short description
- At least one learning fact
- An image path
- A source URL
- Image credit and license information

For the current game, provide at least 10 items. The Basic list is configured separately in `content/game-config.json`.

## Review checklist

- Are the names spelled correctly in every language?
- Are the facts appropriate for the learners' age?
- Does each image have permission or a suitable license?
- Does every source link open and support the claim?
- Are the Basic and Advanced counts correct?
- Does the game still show a total score of 100?
- Has the game been tested on a phone and a computer?

The teacher is the final approver of educational content. AI output should be treated as a draft until reviewed.
